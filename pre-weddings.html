Questions ?

How early should we book?

Ideally 3–4 weeks before the wedding so outfits and locations can be planned comfortably.

Can we shoot outside Harda?

Yes. Call or WhatsApp us with your preferred location and we will share a plan.

How many outfits can we change?

Usually 2–3 looks work best. We will suggest what suits your package and location.

Do you also make a pre-wedding video?

Yes, we can create a cinematic teaser or reel along with your photos.
