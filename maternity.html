Before the shoot ?

A few simple tips make your session easier and your photos even more beautiful.

  • Best time: usually the 7th to 8th month
  • Choose comfortable, flowing outfits
  • Keep water and snacks handy
  • Plan breaks – your comfort comes first
  • Bring your partner and family if you wish

Yes, a cosy home session is a lovely option.
